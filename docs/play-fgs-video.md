# The video Google Play asks for

Play will not let the closed-testing release through until the
`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE` declaration has a video link next to
"Continuous data transfer to an external device". The reviewer wants to see the
service doing the thing the permission is for, and see its notification while it
happens.

The signed 1.0.0 release build is already installed on the phone
(`com.vythera.relay`, not the `.debug` one), so what you record is what was uploaded.

## Before you record

- Relay open and running on the PC, phone and PC already paired and on the same Wi-Fi.
- One big file on the phone: a video of 200 MB or more, so the transfer lasts long
  enough to be filmed. A small file finishes before the notification is readable.
- Record with the phone's own screen recorder (Quick Settings › Screen record).
  Audio doesn't matter. No narration needed.

## What to film, in order

1. **Open Relay** and let the Devices screen sit for two seconds, with the PC
   listed as a connected device. (~3 s)
2. **Tap the PC**, tap **Files** or **Photos**, and pick the big file. (~8 s)
3. The transfer starts. **Pull down the notification shade straight away** and hold
   it there for three or four seconds, so the Relay notification and its progress
   bar are clearly readable. This is the bit the reviewer is looking for. (~5 s)
4. **Press Home.** Stay on the home screen, or open any other app, for about ten
   seconds. The point is that the transfer keeps running with Relay in the
   background. (~10 s)
5. **Pull the shade down again.** The progress has moved on. Hold until the
   transfer finishes and the notification says it is done. (~10 s)
6. **Go back into Relay** and show the finished transfer in the list. If it's easy,
   point the phone at the PC screen for a second to show the file arriving there.
   Nice to have, not required. (~5 s)

Forty to sixty seconds in total. One take is fine; it does not need editing.
